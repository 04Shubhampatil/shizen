About Shizen

A legacy written in wilderness

30+ Years of impact

From the cloud forests of Borneo to the ice fields of Patagonia, Shizen works where biodiversity is richest and the threats are greatest.

For over three decades, Shizen the Japanese word for "nature" has stood as a guardian of the Earth's irreplaceable natural heritage. We unite science, indigenous wisdom, and global community to safeguard the living systems that sustain us all.

We believe natural heritage belongs to all of humanity and to the species, waters, and soils that preceded us by millennia. Our approach blends rigorous conservation science with deep partnerships alongside indigenous communities, governments, and local stewards who have protected these lands for generations.

Every acre we safeguard, every species we pull back from the brink, and every corridor we restore is a thread in the living tapestry we are committed to preserving.
